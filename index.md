# Anansi Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

Anansi is a podcast player for iPhone that skips ads automatically. Ads are detected on your device, and only anonymous descriptions of confirmed ads are shared so other listeners can skip them too. Your listening stays yours.

## What Anansi never collects

- No audio. Episode audio is never uploaded from your device.
- No listening history. Anansi does not send which shows you subscribe to, which episodes you play, or how long you listen.
- No ads, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- No name, email address or sign-in.

## What Anansi shares

When Anansi finds an ad, it can share one of these with the Anansi ad database:

- **For an inserted ad:** an audio fingerprint of the ad, a set of numbers computed from its sound that cannot be turned back into audio.
- **For a host-read ad:** a hash that identifies the episode file, and the start and end times of the ad segment.

You can also vote on whether a shared segment really is an ad. To weight votes and stop abuse, Anansi gives your device a random identifier that is not linked to your name, your Apple ID or your other apps. Submissions and votes are stored with that identifier, and the confirmed ad database is shared with every Anansi user.

Your device also downloads the confirmed ad database to match against episodes locally.

## Podcasts and downloads

To play podcasts, Anansi fetches feeds and episode files directly from each podcast's publisher or host. Those servers see your IP address and the episode requested, as with any podcast app, under their own privacy policies.

## Data on your device

Your subscriptions, downloads, playback progress, transcripts and settings are stored only on your device, inside Anansi's own storage. If you use iCloud device backups, iOS may include this data in your backup, under Apple's own privacy terms. Deleting Anansi removes its data from your device.

## Children

Anansi does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If Anansi ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
